IntroductionWhy upgrade for tomorrow?

Fuel shortages, grid stress, extreme weather and crowded roads are real future risks. Instead of a new model, this seminar suggests practical upgrade ideas for a Toyota family/community EV carrying 6–8 people: keep it running without petrol, protect passengers, and make it safe for the people and planet around it.
